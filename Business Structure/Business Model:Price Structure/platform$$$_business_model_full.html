Optimisation audit — what is and is not yet optimised

Honest assessment of the current business model gaps

Price ladder is well structured
$8 entry in January climbing to $15 in December is correctly calibrated. Low enough to break the first-purchase barrier, high enough at peak to reflect quality and trust. This is optimised.
Platform roles are clearly separated
Instagram drives traffic. OnlyFans monetises. Patreon retains. No platform is trying to do someone else's job. This separation reduces confusion and maximises each platform's native strengths.
Psychology funnel is correctly sequenced
Parasocial relationship before any sell. First PPV after two weeks of trust-building. VIP introduced only after the first PPV is proven. Bundle offered only in established months. Sequence is correct.
Custom content pricing not yet defined
DMs are open and custom requests are mentioned, but there is no defined pricing structure for custom content. This is one of the highest revenue-per-fan mechanisms on OnlyFans. Needs a clear menu: custom photo $20–$40, custom video $50–$100, based on length and specificity.
Mass DM strategy is mentioned but not scripted
Mass DMs on PPV drop days are flagged in the calendar but the actual message templates are not written. A well-timed mass DM with the right opener ("I shot something today and I want you to see it first") can double PPV unlock rate on drop day. Needs scripting.
Patreon $25 tier is absent — revenue gap
The calendar has $5 and $10 tiers but no $25 tier. A $25 tier offering personalised interaction, private polls, and priority custom requests is a proven Patreon retention and revenue tool. Missing this tier leaves money on the table from the highest-value fans.
No tip jar strategy or tip triggers on free posts
Free posts have a Tip button but the calendar does not include any tip-triggering captions. A simple line at the end of high-performing free posts ("if this made your day, tip jar is open below") can generate significant supplemental income from warm followers who are not yet buying PPV.
No re-engagement strategy for lapsed followers
Once someone unfollows or stops engaging, there is currently no re-entry mechanism. A quarterly "welcome back" discounted PPV bundle targeted via Instagram Reels (which surface to non-followers) is a standard recovery tactic. Not in the plan yet.
Instagram conversion rate is untracked
The plan uses IG as a traffic source but has no defined way to measure how many IG visitors become OF followers. Without tracking this, it is impossible to know if the content strategy is working or which post types drive the most OF follows. Need a UTM link or at minimum a weekly follower count log.
